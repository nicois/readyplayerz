readyplayerz

A free first-person zombie survival shooter that runs in your browser. Pick a loadout, choose a map and hold out against waves that never stop, alone or with friends. No download, no account, no sign-up.

Play now

How it works

Zombies come in waves, and every wave is bigger than the last. Kill them all and the next one starts. If you stay clear of them for a while your health comes back, so moving and picking your fights matters as much as aim.

Ammo is limited. Each gun carries a reserve, and ammo boxes spread over the map top it up. Boxes are also the only way to get more grenades. You can sprint to get out of trouble, but sprinting stops a reload, and you can't pick up a box while running.

Solo and online co-op

Solo is you against the waves on the map of your choice.

Online lets up to six players fight the same waves together. Make a room and send your friends the invite link, or type in a friend's four-letter room code. There's no sign-up: pick a name and play.

Questions

Is readyplayerz free?

Yes. It's free to play, with no download, no account and no sign-up.

What do I need to play?

A computer with a keyboard and mouse and a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux. Phones and tablets aren't supported.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Choose Online, make a room and send the invite link, or type in a friend's four-letter room code. Up to six players fight the same waves together.

Does the game end?

No. The waves keep coming and grow each time. The goal is to see how far you can get.
