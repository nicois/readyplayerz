Privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026

readyplayerz is a free browser game made by one person in Finland. There are no accounts, no ads and no cookies, and nothing about you is sold. This page says what is collected when you play and why.

What is collected

  • Game analytics. When the game loads, a random visitor ID is stored in your browser, so a return visit can be told apart from a new one. With it go the pages you open and what happens in play: games started and ended, the wave reached, kills, time played, frame rate, rooms made and joined, and changes to the loadout and settings.
  • Your device. The browser, operating system, screen size and graphics card, and whether the game loaded or failed to, with any error messages, so problems can be found and fixed.
  • Rough location. Your IP address is used to estimate the country and city you're playing from.
  • Online play. The name you choose is shown to the other players in your room. The game server notes rooms opening and closing and the names joining them in its logs, which are kept only briefly.

Your loadout, settings and name are also saved in your browser so they're there next time. They stay on your device.

Why

To see whether the game loads and runs well on people's computers, which parts get played, and whether people come back, so it can be made better. The legal basis is legitimate interest in running and improving the game. None of it is used for advertising.

Who handles it

  • PostHog stores the analytics, on servers in the United States.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and, like any web host, sees your IP address when you load it.
  • Fly.io runs the online game server, in Sweden.

Your choices

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes the visitor ID, and a new one is made next time. You can ask to see, or have deleted, what's held about you by writing to contact@readyplayerz.com. Include the date and roughly the time you played, since there's no account to look you up by. If you think your data is mishandled, you can complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman at tietosuoja.fi.

Children

readyplayerz isn't meant for children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this changes, this page is updated and the date at the top moves.
